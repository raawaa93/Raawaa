## 2. Creative system: one video, one point, one action

Use the **1–1–1 structure** for 15–30 second videos:

1. **Hook (0–2s):** one concrete question, common mistake, close-up or surprising detail.
2. **Value/proof (2–18s):** one approved blend, one point, 2–4 shots. Demonstrate only what the final label and evidence allow.
3. **Close (18–30s):** repeat the product/lesson and give one action only.

Production defaults (test, do not treat as platform ranking guarantees):
- Master: vertical 9:16, ideally 1080×1920; keep text and product away from edge/interface zones.
- Typical cut length: 1–3 seconds where it adds new visual information; avoid edits that exist only for speed.
- Use a clear spoken Arabic voice track plus manually checked Arabic subtitles.
- Record a clean voice-only master; use only music/sounds licensed for the specific platform, account type and territory.
- Produce original footage. Do not cross-post a downloaded, watermarked copy; export a clean master and make native edits/captions for each platform.
- Keep the quiet-luxury visual system restrained: warm ivory, charcoal, stone neutrals, controlled light, close-up texture, deliberate negative space. Do not use gold effects, stock hair, AI bottle images or ornate fonts at the expense of legibility.
- Brand logo is a sign-off, not the opening shot.

## 3. Content pillars

1. **Usage clarity:** different blends have different application zones. Use only current approved instructions.
2. **Small-dose ritual:** quantity and even distribution; never invent a drop count or universal dose.
3. **Real texture and finish:** real product and hair under consistent light; no filters that alter texture, shine or colour.
4. **Label literacy:** product name, actual volume, approved ingredients, directions and warnings; only film the final approved label.
5. **Blend selection:** compare only approved differences; never infer a benefit from an ingredient's reputation.
6. **Trust and service:** verified packing, batch/label details, order steps, payment and delivery information. Protect customer data.
7. **Real customer voice (later):** only with informed written permission, authentic experience, clear incentive disclosure and substantiation for objective claims.

**Rosemary & Caffeine gate:** no application, scalp contact, growth, hair-loss, follicle, circulation or treatment content until the lab/qualified reviewer approves use site, dose, frequency, warnings, final label and claims in writing. Until then, keep any idea internal; do not publish an ingredient-benefit story.

## 5. Example fully specified scripts

### R26-01 — Usage clarity (18–22 seconds)
- 0–2s: Close-up of hair ends; text: «مو كل مزيج يُستخدم بنفس الطريقة».
- 2–6s: Split screen with two neutral text cards: «زيت الزيتون والبانثينول» / «الخروع والزيوت المختارة».
- 6–14s: Show direction cards only: «من منتصف الشعر إلى الأطراف» / «على الأطراف فقط — كمية قليلة جداً».
- 14–19s: Brand end card: «اقرئي تعليمات كل عبوة».
- Voice: «مو كل مزيج إله نفس طريقة الاستخدام. زيت الزيتون والبانثينول من منتصف الشعر للأطراف، ومزيج الخروع على الأطراف فقط وبكمية قليلة جداً. تعليمات العبوة هي المرجع.»
- Caption: «طريقة الاستخدام تختلف حسب المزيج. اقرئي تعليمات العبوة قبل الاستخدام.»
- CTA: «اكتبي اسم المزيج الذي تريدين معرفة تفاصيله.»
- Do not show Rosemary & Caffeine or any scalp application.

### R26-02 — Olive Oil & Panthenol (20–24 seconds)
- 0–2s: Actual final pack enters frame; text: «من وين نوزّعه؟».
- 2–5s: Label and blend name in focus.
- 5–10s: Small amount on palms; no fixed drop count.
- 10–17s: Apply from mid-lengths to ends on damp or dry hair, following approved directions.
- 17–22s: Natural movement under fixed light; no before/after claim.
- Voice: «مزيج زيت الزيتون والبانثينول: ابدئي بكمية صغيرة، ووزعيه من منتصف الشعر إلى الأطراف على شعر رطب أو جاف، حسب تعليمات العبوة.»
- Caption: «ابدئي بالقليل واتبعي تعليمات العبوة.»
- CTA: «اسألي عن الأحجام المتاحة.»

### R26-03 — Castor blend (18–22 seconds)
- 0–2s: Close-up of ends; text: «الأطراف فقط».
- 2–6s: Show the actual final pack and label.
- 6–12s: Show a very small amount on fingertips.
- 12–18s: Distribute gently on ends only.
- 18–22s: End card with blend name and CTA.
- Voice: «مزيج الخروع والزيوت المختارة مخصص للأطراف فقط. ابدئي بكمية قليلة جداً ووزعيها برفق، دون إفراط.»
- Caption: «لا تعممي طريقة الاستخدام بين المزيجات.»
- CTA: «أرسلي سؤالك عن هذا المزيج.»

## 7. Claims, testimonials and AI media

- EU cosmetic claims common criteria (Regulation 655/2013): legal compliance, truthfulness, evidential support, honesty, fairness and informed decisions. Claims about ingredients do not automatically prove the finished formula: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2013/655/oj/eng
- UK ASA before/after guidance: retain dated, signed evidence that images are genuine and unmanipulated; visual impression must not exaggerate efficacy: https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/before-and-after-photos.html
- US FDA: claims to treat/prevent disease or affect body structure/function can cause a cosmetic to be regulated as a drug: https://www.fda.gov/labeling-claims
- US FTC endorsements: the advertiser is responsible for claims made through endorsements, and testimonials do not replace substantiation: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-255.2
- These are jurisdiction-specific references, not a statement of Syrian law. Obtain local label/advertising review before launch.

Green only if supported by the finished product: appearance/feel claims such as shine, softer feel, smoother-looking ends, detangling, pack volume and label directions.
Amber: percentages, duration claims, clinical/dermatologist tested, breakage reduction, sensitive scalp suitability, organic/natural, comparisons, before/after.
Red until approved and substantiated: hair growth/regrowth, stopping hair loss, treating scalp/dandruff, activating follicles, circulation, repairing structural damage, guaranteed results and medical language.

Disclose paid, gifted or affiliate creator relationships clearly in Arabic and with the platform's tool where available. Label realistic AI-generated or materially altered people, voices, events or results when platform policy requires it. Never create fake customers, testimonials, clinical experts, product packs or hair results.
- TikTok AI content label: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/ai-generated-content-label
- YouTube altered/synthetic disclosure: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15447836

## 8. Phone-only production workflow

1. **Preflight:** approved label, use directions, claim sheet, actual pack/stock, price and order terms.
2. **Batch scripts:** 3–4 scripts per shoot; each one point and one CTA. Keep Rosemary & Caffeine blocked.
3. **Set:** rear phone camera, clean lens, window light at 45°, white card reflector, stable tripod/books, neutral ivory background.
4. **Capture:** 2–4 second takes, three hook alternatives, clean voice-over, actual hands/pack/hair only. Do not show private customer data.
5. **Edit:** 9:16, manual Arabic subtitle check, clean voice above music, no watermark, safe margins. Keep a voice-only master.
6. **Rights/quality:** clear music, footage, model releases and creator permissions; check AI disclosure; proofread Arabic on a phone.
7. **Publish:** native platform adaptation only after the correct brand account is connected and the owner approves the specific content. Otherwise deliver files and copy; do not publish.
8. **Archive:** raw clips, consent, music licence, script version, final export, evidence and publication URL.

Free-first tools already in the studio workflow: phone camera/Photos, VN Video Editor (free functions only), Canva free elements, native Instagram/Facebook editors and YouTube Studio. Avoid subscriptions and trial-to-paid conversions. Verify app access/feature availability in Syria before relying on any one editor.

## 9. Attribution and experiment design

Assign each creative a unique ID, e.g. `IG-A-R26-02`, `FB-B-R26-03`. Use separate UTMs:
`utm_source=instagram|facebook|youtube`
`utm_medium=organic_social`
`utm_campaign=rawaa_blend_launch_2026`
`utm_content=R26-02`

Use unique creator codes; never share one code across creators. The order log should record creative ID, first-touch, qualified inquiry, blend, city, order, dispatch, delivery/refusal, collected amount, delivery cost, discounts, creator fee and contribution margin. If UTM is lost in WhatsApp, ask one fixed attribution question and reconcile with code/time. Do not put customer names, phone numbers or addresses in GitHub.

Funnel:
- Attention: 3-second hold, average watch time, completion, rewatches.
- Intent: saves/shares, profile visits, destination clicks, DMs.
- Qualified lead: genuine product/size/order question, not an emoji or passive view.
- Outcome: confirmed → dispatched → delivered and paid → contribution margin.

Core formulas:
- Inquiry-to-order = confirmed orders / qualified inquiries.
- Delivery rate = delivered orders / dispatched orders.
- Delivered conversion = delivered orders / attributable unique sessions.
- Cost per delivered order = attributable production + creator + media cost / delivered orders.
- Contribution per creative = collected revenue − product + packaging + delivery + discount + returns + creator + production + media costs.

Testing sequence: Week 1 hook variants; Week 2 proof style; Week 3 CTA/destination; Week 4 repeat the best combination. Change one major variable at a time. With low volume, report directional evidence, not statistical certainty. Select winners by delivered orders and contribution, not views alone.

## 10. Approval checklist before a post can become publishable

- [ ] Correct blend, final pack, label, size and stock confirmed.
- [ ] Directions, claims and warnings approved for that blend.
- [ ] No unsubstantiated therapeutic or performance claims.
- [ ] No fabricated testimonials, results or product photography.
- [ ] Consent and rights for every person, voice, clip and sound.
- [ ] AI disclosure completed where required.
- [ ] Arabic spelling, spacing, RTL text, captions and mobile legibility checked.
- [ ] CTA, destination, current price and delivery/payment terms tested.
- [ ] Unique UTM/creator code assigned and order attribution ready.
- [ ] Brand owner approval recorded for the specific final export.
